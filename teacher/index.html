Set up this device

Do this once per phone or tablet. The letter must appear at its true physical size, so the screen has to be measured first.

1 · Measure the screen
Match a bank card to this box
Any bank, ATM or student card works — they are all 85.6 mm wide. Slide until the box is exactly as wide as the card.
2 · Testing distance
3 · Screening gate
Letter height & width
8.73 mm
W = 12 × 0.0014547 × (3 ÷ 6)
Letters are drawn on a 5 × 5 grid — stroke width is one fifth of the letter height — so the whole letter subtends 5 minutes of arc at the child's eye and each stroke subtends 1 minute.

Before you tap start
  1. Child stands at the 3 m mark, facing the screen.
  2. Child covers the left eye with their palm — cupped, not pressed.
  3. Child says each letter out loud. You tap what they say.
  4. Both eyes stay open behind the palm. No squinting, no peeking.
